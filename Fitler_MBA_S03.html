The reframe

Your product is not your business.

A business model is how a company creates value, delivers it, and captures some back as money. Same product, different model - and the outcomes split.

The model made it
Gillette
Razor cheap. Blades forever.
Salesforce
Rent the software, don't sell a box.
Amazon
Thin margins, the flywheel, Prime + AWS.
Nike
Owns the brand. Outsources the factory.
The model broke it
WeWork
Long leases, short memberships, priced like software.
Windows Phone
No apps, no users, no apps. The market never formed.

Every piece of a model is a bet - some you know cold, some you're quietly guessing. Hold that thought.

How the money comes in

Revenue Streams & Structure

One-Time Sale
a car · a laptop · a Torev motor
You sell the thing once. Simple, but you're always hunting the next sale.
Subscription
Netflix · the gym · your phone plan
A recurring fee for ongoing access. Predictable - if they don't churn.
Transaction / Marketplace
Airbnb · eBay · Uber
You take a cut of every deal you enable. Scales with volume, not headcount.
SaaS / Per-Seat
Salesforce · Slack
Software priced by the user or by usage. Lands and expands inside an account.
Licensing / Royalty
Dolby · ARM · branded IP
A fee per unit someone else ships. You monetize the idea, not the factory.
Hands up - which of these is Torev? Call it out.
