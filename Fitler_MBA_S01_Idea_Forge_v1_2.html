Stage 01 · Capture the idea

Write the one-liner.

A problem you've seen, a frustration you've had, a market you can't stop thinking about. No editing, no business plan — just one honest sentence. We'll sharpen it from there.

Don't perfect it. The forge handles the rough edges.
Stage 02 · Answer the sharpening questions

Now Claude interviews you.

Four Foundation Questions, two or three sharpening questions each. Answer what you can; the gaps are also data. The point is to find out what you actually believe.
